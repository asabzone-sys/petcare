Triage Assessment

Identify Your Pet's Urgency Level

Match your pet's current symptoms against standardized veterinary triage tiers.

RED TIER IMMEDIATE (0 MINS)

Critical / Life Threatening

Rush to emergency veterinary facility immediately:

  • Severe dyspnea / choking / blue gums
  • Unconscious or unresponsive collapse
  • Continuous seizures lasting > 3 mins
  • Hit by car / arterial pulsing bleeding
  • Male cat unable to urinate (screaming in box)
  • Gastric Dilation Volvulus (bloat / dry heaving)
AMBER TIER URGENT (1 - 2 HOURS)

Urgent Evaluation Required

Contact clinic and prepare for same-day visit:

  • Ingestion of rodenticide / toxic lily / chocolate
  • Multiple episodes of acute vomiting & lethargy
  • Sudden non-weight bearing lameness
  • Eye trauma / squinting / corneal cloudiness
  • Acute allergic facial swelling or hives
  • Deep laceration requiring surgical sutures
GREEN TIER SAME-WEEK CHECK

Non-Emergency Care

Schedule standard clinical consultation:

  • Mild ear scratching or shaking head
  • Single soft stool episode with normal energy
  • Minor skin flaking or seasonal itch
  • Routine booster vaccines & dental scales
  • Microchip implantation or wellness exam
  • Dietary prescription refills
First Aid Action

Step-by-Step Pet CPR Protocol

Follow the RECOVER initiative cardiopulmonary resuscitation steps.

Check Responsiveness & Airway

Call the pet's name loudly and pinch a paw pad firmly. If unresponsive, gently extend the neck, open the mouth, pull the tongue forward, and inspect for foreign obstruction (balls, bone fragments).

Position on Right Lateral Side

Lay the pet on a firm, flat surface with their right side down (left side facing upward, where the apex of the cardiac silhouette is located directly beneath the elbow point).

Chest Compressions (100–120 bpm)

Interlock hands over the widest part of the ribcage. Compress 1/3 to 1/2 the width of the chest to the beat of "Stayin' Alive". Allow full chest recoil between compressions without bouncing.

Rescue Breathing (30:2 Ratio)

Close pet's mouth firmly and seal lips with your hands. Place your mouth over the pet's nostrils and deliver 2 quick rescue breaths until the chest gently rises. Deliver 30 compressions followed by 2 breaths.
