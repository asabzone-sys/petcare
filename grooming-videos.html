Coat Brushing All Breeds

Detailed step-by-step masterclass demonstrating calm grooming routines.

Step-by-Step Technique Guide

  1. Inspect coat and detangle gently.
  2. Apply calm conditioning spray.
  3. Brush in line sections following fur growth.
  4. Reward with healthy training treats.
Fear-Free Technique Tip: Always desensitize equipment sound first. Keep sessions under 10 minutes and reward with high-value treats to create positive emotional associations.
