Detailed Medical Case Studies

Demonstration surgical histories, diagnostic ultrasound summaries, and recovery protocols.

Bella (German Shepherd)

Recovered

Chief Complaint: Acute non-weight bearing lameness on right hind leg.

Diagnosis: Complete Cranial Cruciate Ligament (CCL) Rupture with Meniscal Tear.
Protocol: TPLO surgery, Carprofen analgesia, low-impact laser rehab.

Outcome: 90% full weight-bearing restored at 6-week radiographic check.

Simba (Domestic Long Hair)

Resolved

Chief Complaint: Stranguria, hematuria, and vocalization in litter box for 18h.

Diagnosis: Feline Idiopathic Cystitis (FIC) with Obstructive Struvite Urolithiasis.
Protocol: Atraumatic catheterization, IV fluid diuresis, prescription wet S/O diet.

Outcome: Normal voluntary urination established; zero recurrence over 60 days.

Archie (Cavalier Spaniel)

Maintenance

Chief Complaint: Severe chronic bilateral ear pruritus and paw licking.

Diagnosis: Atopic Dermatitis with Secondary Malassezia Otitis Externa.
Protocol: Deep ear flushing, Cytopoint monthly injection, sublingual immunotherapy.

Outcome: 80% pruritus score reduction within 72h; clear follow-up ear cytology.
